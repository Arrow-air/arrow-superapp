Catch people when they show up, and keep what we learn about them

X brings people to Discord, the site and the repos. Nothing writes them down. These mockups add one link every way in points to, a three-minute profile, a registry the team can search, intro calls you start, and notes only the team can see.

Built on what Gavin already made

  • The disciplines taxonomy v1.0 (9 groups, 43 disciplines, community-approved in July) is the skill list everywhere here.
  • His contributor map (website issue #180) becomes the registry's public face. The registry is the store; the map reads people who chose to be on it. His open PR #254 (a live grid of everyone with commits in Arrow's repos) is the start of the earned layer.
  • His September onboarding wizard (website branch sl33ty/explore/superapp) is the pattern for Join: one big question a screen, contribute or just follow, disciplines, experience, place, time, how you heard. His quests become the checklist on Your Arrow, without token rewards (Q28).
  • Your Sept 26 profile note: said, linked and earned stay separate, the "something you built and what went wrong" question, and per-field visibility.

Intro calls

People opt in on their profile ("open to a 20-minute intro call"). Nobody gets a call by asking an agent; a person on the team picks them and writes to them.

MY PICK

A · You reach out

A queue of people who opted in, best signal first. You (or a lead) send a DM in your own words with two or three times.

  • Your time goes to people you chose
  • A message from you is the welcome
  • Only as fast as you write them

B · They book

A booking link on the done screen for people with a finished profile and one work link, capped at a few slots a week.

  • Scales without you
  • Your calendar fills with whoever clicks first
  • Feels like a sales funnel

C · Weekly welcome call

Gavin's quest: one open call a week for everyone new, run by anyone on the team. Earns the Member role.

  • Everyone meets a human in week one
  • Shows you who to pick for A
  • Group calls hide quiet people

A and C together: C for everyone, A for the people you want to know. B stays off unless the queue outgrows you.
