# Meeting consensus UI
Status: seed
Raised by: Gavin and Thomas, 2026-09-17 (~54:30–57:00)

## The idea
After a call, an agent turns the questions posed into a simple review UI (Reddit board, feed, or swipe) where everyone marks agree/disagree, producing a consensus readout. Render the same decision stream as Reddit, Twitter, or Tinder depending on taste.

## Why it matters
Meetings are tedious and rarely yield clean decisions. A quick post-meeting pass extracts them. Also a cheap first prototype.

## How it might work
Transcript to questions to a swipe/vote page. Could be tried on this repo's OPEN-QUESTIONS.md immediately.

## Risks and tensions
Multiple renderings is 'massive scope.' Keep one.
