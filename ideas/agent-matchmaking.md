# Agent matchmaking
Status: seed
Raised by: Gavin, 2026-09-17 (~30:00–35:00)

## The idea
Personal agents that know your skills and preferences act as a matchmaker between people who need help on a project and people who could give it. Humans evaluate the proposed match and remain the arbiter of direction.

## Why it matters
Historically the hardest part: if either the decision-maker or the potential contributor doesn't buy into matching, nothing happens. Agents can shoulder that.

## How it might work
Agents converse to allocate broad bucket tasks, propose splits (e.g. 20/80), surface tasks in a personal dashboard. Bar is 'good enough,' not perfect.

## Risks and tensions
Same-model convergence. Does it coordinate us better than the time it costs? Nobody has seen a compelling multi-agent coordination demo yet.
