# The decision ladder
Status: exploring
Raised by: Thomas, journal 9/10; presented 2026-09-17

## The idea
Big decisions (fund an aircraft) work via forum and Snapshot. Small ones belong to whoever is building. Medium decisions (layout, PCB specs, attachment power budget) currently fall to one project lead and have no governance home.

## Why it matters
Medium decisions carry real expenses and affect token holders in different workshops with different needs. One lead can't hold all the information as Arrow grows, and a manned aircraft multiplies interdependent decisions.

## How it might work
A lightweight, weighted vote or thread-based consensus per medium decision inside the app. Lead retains an opinionated final call on trade-offs; the community supplies information and preferences. See weighted-voting.md and spec-thread-to-bounty.md.

## Risks and tensions
Aircraft are trade-offs; too much distributed input produces a compromise machine that does nothing well. Leads must be open to compromise but someone has to be opinionated.
