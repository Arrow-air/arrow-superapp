# Manufacturer gating and traceable identity
Status: seed
Raised by: Gavin, 2026-09-17 (~08:20)

## The idea
Most things open, but manufacturer sections gated by permission level with a traceable, soulbound-style identity so support discussions are attributable and nobody takes designs off to do shady things untraced.

## Why it matters
Manufacturers need support and accountability. Open designs still need a chain of who asked what.

## How it might work
Permission tiers, soulbound credential tied to profile. Separate manufacturer dashboard.

## Risks and tensions
Tension with open-source ethos. Identity systems are hard to get right.
