# Own governance system replacing Snapshot
Status: seed
Raised by: Gavin, 2026-09-17 (~04:35)

## The idea
Build Arrow's own voting layer instead of Snapshot: micro-votes, quick decisions, quadratic voting experiments.

## Why it matters
Snapshot is too clunky for many small decisions. A playground for coordination mechanics.

## How it might work
Months of experimenting and failing expected. Could layer on Snapshot for big votes and own the small ones.

## Risks and tensions
Rebuilding governance infra is a lot of surface for a small team. Security of token voting.
