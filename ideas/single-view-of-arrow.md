# One picture of Arrow right now
Status: exploring
Raised by: Thomas journal 9/12; Gavin's DAO dashboard 2026-09-17

## The idea
A single place where anyone can see what's happening across Arrow and find a way to contribute. Today state is split between GitHub, Fusion, Discord, calls, and memory.

## Why it matters
You can't govern or contribute to state you can't see. Both presenters independently landed on this. It is also the least token-dependent piece.

## How it might work
Aggregate GitHub activity, Discord threads, meeting notes, open tasks, and project status. Gavin adds: leaderboard, support tickets anyone can pick up, governance traffic lights for permissioned contributors, accountability view of gated project deliverables.

## Risks and tensions
Wendell Berry test: adding another tool can dilute attention further. Must be a demonstrable improvement over Discord plus GitHub.
