# Contributor buckets, not experts
Status: seed
Raised by: Gavin, 2026-09-17 (~13:00–19:00)

## The idea
Classify contributors by aptitude and interest (testers, researchers, assemblers, organizers) rather than expert vs. non-expert. Experts sit as project leads shaping the brief and preserving cohesion; anyone with an agent can deliver work that fulfills the brief.

## Why it matters
Individuals are 10–100x more productive but integration and coordination are failing. Traditional expert-owns-everything doesn't scale to that.

## How it might work
Bucket tags on profiles. Leads outline requirements and shape; bucketed contributors fill in. Agents route tasks by bucket.

## Risks and tensions
Loses some cohesion and spirit an expert brings. Needs a trust ladder so enthusiasm isn't mistaken for reliability. Thomas wants to sit with it.
