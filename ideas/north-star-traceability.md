# North Star traceability
Status: seed
Raised by: Gavin, 2026-09-17 (~03:40)

## The idea
A source of truth at the top. Every decision traces back to the roadmap or AAPs. Possibly per-area mini-roadmaps. 'Gated values' the way gated projects work.

## Why it matters
Lets people disagree less and point back to the highest-value thing instead.

## How it might work
Decisions and proposals carry a link to a roadmap item or value. Untraced proposals are flagged.

## Risks and tensions
Bureaucratic if enforced too hard. Roadmaps go stale.
