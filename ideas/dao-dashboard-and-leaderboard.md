# Public DAO dashboard and leaderboard
Status: seed
Raised by: Gavin, 2026-09-17 (~00:00–03:30)

## The idea
A public dashboard: live activity, global leaderboard of contributions, contributor rankings by $ARROW earned per period, ongoing work, open support tickets, tokenomics and sales tab, accountability view of gated project deliverables.

## Why it matters
Transparency, inspiration, and public accountability. People deliver when they know others are watching what's due.

## How it might work
Overlaps heavily with single-view-of-arrow.md; this file holds the leaderboard, accountability, and sales/tokenomics pieces specifically.

## Risks and tensions
Leaderboards reward volume over value. Public accountability can shame rather than motivate.
