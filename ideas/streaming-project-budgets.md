# Streaming $ARROW budgets
Status: seed
Raised by: Thomas journal 9/12

## The idea
A fixed monthly or streaming budget of $ARROW flows through the app, possibly allocated per project (Spearhead gets N tokens a month), and governance distributes it to the people with the most impact. Eventually all project funding flows this way.

## Why it matters
Makes the A in DAO real. Reduces manual treasury work and discrete funding votes.

## How it might work
Treasury stream into per-project buckets. Distribution driven by retro allocation and weighted votes. Rules for who can be paid and audit trail.

## Risks and tensions
Biggest slop attack surface. Autonomy before the allocation signal is trustworthy will pay the wrong people. Needs bounded circles.
