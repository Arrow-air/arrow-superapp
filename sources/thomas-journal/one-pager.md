# How could we make decisions together at Arrow?

*Thomas · September 2026. Some ideas for an Arrow app. I’d like to work through these together on the call.*

## 1. Where decisions get stuck

Once we know what to build, getting it done can be as simple as a prompt or a bounty. I think the harder part is agreeing on what we need and working out the specs.

| Decision | Example | How it works today |
|---|---|---|
| Project funding | Should we fund a new aircraft? | The forum works well for big decisions like this. |
| Design decisions | What aircraft layout or PCB specs do we need? | These tend to fall to one project lead. Snapshot is too clunky for lots of decisions at this level. |

We have token holders in different workshops with different needs. One project lead can’t have all that information. These choices also involve real expenses, so I think we need an easier way for token holders to help make them.

## 2. What the app could help us do

**See what’s happening and where to help.** Bring together the project information now spread across GitHub, Discord, meeting notes, and people’s memory. Anyone should be able to see what’s underway and find a way to contribute.

**Discuss designs with the CAD models.** Integrate our CAD models into the app so people can look at the design while discussing it.

**Find people and see their past work.** Give people profiles with a bio, skills, and location. A reputation or ranking system could help people show what they’ve done and what they’re good at.

**Make decisions as the work progresses.** I’m imagining governance that happens throughout a project, with less manual coordination around each vote. People can’t vote constantly, so maybe their AI agents could act as delegates and vote on their behalf.

## 3. Paying for the work

**Pay for working out the specs as well as building.** The people who do the creative work of figuring out what to make should be rewarded too.

**Work out how to reward each contribution.** Reddit-style comments and voting could help, perhaps with token-weighted votes. But I might upvote both a quick comment and a detailed analysis without valuing them equally. Maybe people should explicitly allocate reward tokens afterward, as in Coordinape.

**Vote on grants and bounties.** Use voting to choose who takes on a funded task. We could also award grants after work is done, with care to check that the work was useful.

**Manage funding through the app.** Set a fixed monthly budget of $ARROW, or a budget paid out continuously over time, to reward work and ideas. I’d like as much of this as possible to run automatically, with rules for deciding who gets paid. The app could also automate our other project funding.

## 4. Apps I have in mind

I’m imagining a combination of these:

| Reference | What I have in mind |
|---|---|
| GitHub | Project work and its history |
| Discourse | Longer discussions and proposals |
| Reddit | Comments and voting on ideas |
| Snapshot | Token-holder voting |
| Coordinape | Allocating rewards after contributions |
| Dework | Tasks, bounties, and assignments |

## 5. Questions for the call

1. Which decisions are hardest to make together today?
2. How could people in different workshops help work out shared specs?
3. How should we value contributions and check work before paying for it?
4. What would we want our AI agents to do as delegates?
5. How should a monthly or streaming $ARROW budget be allocated?
6. What would make this useful for following a project or joining in?
