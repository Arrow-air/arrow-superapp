## 9/10 — Office — Arrow Coordination

We need something that makes it easier for our broad community to make decisions about what to build at every level. The actual work is easy once well scoped → usually just a prompt, maybe a bounty.

Our current forum works well for big decisions, like to fund a big aircraft project, but there are a lot of medium decisions like aircraft layout, PCB specs, etc. that are just forced to a single project lead. But forcing decisions to a single project lead isn't ideal when we have many different token holders in different workshops with different needs. Snapshot governance is too clunky for a ton of smaller decisions, and a single person will never have all of the best info to make a good decision quickly. So we'll need some form of token governance, especially since these things will have real expenses associated.

If the hard part is actually coming to the decision or spec on what to make, we'll need a way to reward those that put in the work on that front. It could be reddit-style comments and voting, but maybe with token voting. Although you might upvote a simple comment and a complicated one, those should probably be weighted differently. Maybe Coordinape style, where you explicitly allocate your retro reward tokens.

## 9/12 — Pavilion — More Arrow Coordination

I guess the app is some combo of GitHub, Discourse forum, Coordinape, Reddit, Snapshot, Dework.

We'll want to bring token funding to reward the hard creative work of figuring things out and then actually building them. We can have some sort of ranking/reputation system so people can show off their skills and credibility from previous work. There should be some voting mechanic to assign someone to a task, like if we are awarding them a grant/bounty. Retro grants would be good too, as long as we're diligent to not fund slop.

We're making the leap from manual, discrete governance to something more continuous/autonomous. It might be impractical for humans to be constantly voting, but maybe people's AI agents could serve as their delegates.

This app should also be a good place for anyone to get a picture of what is happening at Arrow right now, and figure out a way that they might be able to contribute. Right now that state is split between GitHub, Discord, and meeting notes / people's memory.

I'm imagining that this app is more richly integrated with our CAD models and things like that, so people can have better discussions around the actual designs.

We could have a fixed monthly or streaming budget of $ARROW token that flows through the app as autonomously as possible to compensate people for their work and ideas. The app will steward good governance to make sure that we're actually compensating the right people.

We should also use this app to automate all of our other project funding flow.

People should be able to make a profile/bio with their skills and location.
